FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers for institutions evaluating ReLearn Skills as a training partner.

Who are the training programs designed for?

Our training solutions are designed for colleges and institutions seeking to strengthen the technical, aptitude and placement readiness of their final-year students.

Can the training be tailored to our students' requirements?

Yes. We work with institutions to align the learning plan with student proficiency, academic schedules, preferred technologies and placement objectives.

What subjects does ReLearn Skills cover?

Our sessions cover technical foundations, programming, aptitude, logical reasoning, project-based learning and interview preparation, based on the agreed institutional requirements.

How are trainers selected for an engagement?

Trainers are shortlisted according to subject expertise, teaching experience and the scope of the engagement. Institutions may evaluate the proposed delivery through a demo session before finalizing the plan.

How do we begin a partnership with ReLearn Skills?

Share your institution's requirements through the enquiry form or contact our team directly. We will discuss your objectives and recommend an appropriate training approach.
